Spent a slow evening walking the Old Port as the light dropped over the
St. Lawrence — here's the honest version, not the postcard one.

## The place

{{< figure src="photo-1.jpg" alt="Cobblestone street in the Old Port" caption="Rue de la Commune at golden hour" >}}

Old Montreal is touristy, yes — but the waterfront after 6pm, once the day-trip
crowds thin out, is genuinely one of the best free things in the city.

## What I did

Walked from Place Jacques-Cartier down to the Clock Tower, grabbed a coffee, and
watched the sunset from the pier.

{{< figure src="photo-2.jpg" alt="Sunset over the St. Lawrence" caption="The Clock Tower pier" >}}

## Recommendations

- **Go for:** the sunset waterfront walk, free and uncrowded after 6pm
- **Try:** a coffee from the kiosk near the Clock Tower
- **Skip:** the overpriced terrace restaurants on Place Jacques-Cartier
- **Budget:** $0–15 / person
- **Best time:** weekday evenings, golden hour
- **Getting there:** Métro Champ-de-Mars (Orange line), 8 min walk
