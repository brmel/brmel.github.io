I develop and maintain industrial machine vision algorithms — geometry,
calibration, edge measurement — and take them from prototype to deployment,
classical methods and deep learning models alike. That work is in C++ at Zebra
Technologies in Montréal, on the Aurora Imaging Library, and at Matrox Imaging
before the two became one company. C++, robotics, AI and machine vision are
what I stay curious about off the clock too, and most of what I build and write
here comes out of that.
