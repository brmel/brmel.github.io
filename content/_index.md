I am a software engineer. I work in C++ on image processing, machine vision and
robotics.

At work my algorithms ship inside industrial products that run on factory
floors. On my own time I build robots, and small applications for people who
asked me for them.

What I enjoy most is the edge cases: the input nobody planned for, the one that
only fails on the fourth run. The write-ups here include what did not work.
