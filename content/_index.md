Zebra after the acquisition. I developed the 2D and 3D vision algorithms in the
Aurora Imaging Library, Zebra's machine vision SDK. Inside it, I took machine
learning from prototype to production, evaluated deep learning models for
object and text detection, and fitted the algorithms to smart cameras with
little memory. Today I also work on generative AI.
