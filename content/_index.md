I like programming, and the edge cases are the part I like most — the input
nobody planned for, the one that only fails on the fourth run.

Professionally that means image processing: designing algorithms and shipping
them into products that run on factory floors. On my own time it means robotics,
and small applications built for people who actually needed them.

The write-ups here include what didn't work.
