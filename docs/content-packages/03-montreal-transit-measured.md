## The claim it must end on

1,960 origin–destination pairs, door to door: at 8 a.m. on a weekday the
median trip takes 45 minutes by car and 61 by transit (1.4×); on a Sunday
afternoon it takes 28 against 66 (2.3×). Transit kept up with the car on
23% of weekday trips — and loses the most across a river (+18 minutes).
