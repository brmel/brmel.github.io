# Decision pending: how Rekba and TikiPro are presented here

Status: **open — founder decision.** Raised by the consolidation sprint
(issue #23, workstream 6). Nothing on the site changes until this is
decided; this note is the whole deliverable.

## The question

Rekba and TikiPro are live products with their own brands, audiences and
store listings. They also appear here as two of the nine project pages,
inside the homepage project flow, because they are his work. Should a
personal portfolio keep carrying full project pages for products that
have public identities of their own?

## The options

**A. Keep them as they are.** Both pages stay, in the homepage flow and
on `/projects/`, unchanged.

**B. De-emphasize from the homepage flow.** Both pages stay live and
listed on `/projects/`; they leave the homepage cards and the featured
order, so the homepage leads with the engineering proof (Farkad,
MemoryTracer, K-Means).

**C. Remove both project pages.** The pages come down, with redirects to
each product's own site, and the portfolio keeps only the work that has
no other home.

## Trade-offs

| | A. Keep | B. De-emphasize | C. Remove |
|---|---|---|---|
| Recruiter proof | Strongest: two shipped products with live users, one click from the homepage | Kept, one click deeper | Lost from the portfolio; the code and stores remain, unlinked from here |
| Audience separation | Product users and recruiters share one domain and one voice | Partial: the homepage reads as an engineer's site first | Cleanest: each product speaks only on its own channels |
| Search | The project pages rank for the product names and funnel readers to the stores | Ranking kept; homepage internal links lost | Ranking passes to the products' own sites, after a transition dip |
| Effort and risk | None | A small ordering change | Redirects, plus the SEO transition |

## What decides it

Whether the portfolio's one job — the engineer brand landing in a
recruiter's first 30 seconds — is worth more with the two products in
the flow, or cleaner without them. The sprint takes no position; the
facts it verified are that both pages are live, linked, and carry
metrics (Rekba: 281 stations, 374 connections) that no other page
repeats.
