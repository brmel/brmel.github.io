A quiet table by the window

Eleven tables, warm bread, and a cod that earns the detour — if you take the early seating and sit by the glass.

Body copy & links

The room is small — eleven tables — and it fills by half past six. I came for the cod and stayed for the bread, which arrives warm and disappears faster than is dignified. The natural-wine list is short and confident, the kind a place keeps when it actually drinks what it pours.

One con, and it's a real one: after seven the playlist starts fighting the room, and the table by the window — the one worth booking — sits closest to the speaker. Go early. Reserve the glass, order the thing the server seems nervous about, and leave before the second seating turns it loud.
