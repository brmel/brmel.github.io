How we checked

  1. Canadian, European and American weather services forecast every hour of the week ahead for two spots in Montréal.
  2. Every 5 minutes we save their latest forecasts, while a weather station beside each spot records what really happens.
  3. Each forecast is then scored against the station's reading for the hour it was about.

Parc Henri-Julien has no station of its own, so it is checked against the nearest one, 7.3 km away.

How each forecast is scored

  • Forecasts are read every 5 minutes, and a copy is saved only when one of the services has changed its mind.
  • Each place is checked by its nearest station: Parc Henri-Julien by McTavish station, 7.3 km away, and Trudeau airport by Trudeau station, 0 km away.
  • The truth for a moment is the station's own reading at that moment, not an hourly average.
  • Only forecasts published before that moment are scored. A value restated afterwards is hindsight, and is never counted.
  • At +6 h, a forecast is scored on what was on record six hours before the hour it describes.
  • Rainfall is compared against the hour it fell in, which the station reports an hour later.
  • Sunshine stands in for cloud cover, which neither station measures.
  • If no reading exists within 5 minutes of the moment, the forecast is left unscored.
