Transit or Car in Greater Montréal

1. The Map: How Much Longer Transit Takes

I asked Google Maps how long 1,960 trips take by car and by transit, from 100 places across Montréal, Laval and both shores to 20 places people go every day: downtown, the universities, the hospitals, the airport and the big malls. Each place takes the median of its trips, and its colour spreads a few kilometres around it.

2. Trip by Trip

Transit was as fast as the car on 97 trips (5%). On 65% it took at least twice as long, and on 26% more than three times as long.

3. Where You Start

From central Montréal, transit takes 1.8× the car time. From the outer South Shore it takes 3.2×: 98 minutes against 31 by car.

4. Where You Go

Berri-UQAM / Quartier Latin is the easiest destination to reach, at 49 minutes by transit in the median trip. Brossard / Du Quartier REM / DIX30 is the hardest, at 90.
