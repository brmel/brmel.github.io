Transit or Car in Greater Montréal

1. The Map: How Much Longer Transit Takes

I asked Google Maps how long 1,960 trips take by car and by transit, from 100 places across Montréal, Laval and both shores to 20 places people go every day: downtown, the universities, the hospitals, the airport and the big malls. I asked twice, for a weekday at 8 a.m. and for a Sunday afternoon. Each place takes the median of its trips, and its colour spreads a few kilometres around it.

2. Rush Hour or Sunday

At 8 a.m. on a weekday, transit takes 1.4× the car time in the median trip, and it was as fast as the car on 444 trips (23%). On a Sunday afternoon the roads are free and buses run less often: 2.3×, and 97 trips (5%).
