How Big Tech Posts on LinkedIn

6 Oct 2026 to 4 Nov 2026 · 99 companies · 2,585 posts

Does the hour a company posts change how the post does?

±3%

Across five parts of the day, a post earns 0.97 to 1.02 times what the same company's usual post earns. For 30 days I read the LinkedIn page of 99 tech companies to see when they post, how often and in what format, and whether any of it moves engagement.

When they post

Mostly in office hours, read on the clock of each company's head office. The busiest hour is a tie, Tuesday and Wednesday at 2 p.m., with 48 posts each, under 2% of the total.

The hour

Busy hours don't earn more. To take page size out, I compared every post with the median post of its own company, so 1.00 is a usual post for that company. Days run from 0.89 on Saturday to 1.11 on Tuesday, and the part of the day barely shows.

The minute

20% of posts went out at exactly :00 and 29% on a quarter-hour, where chance would give 2% and 7%. I can't see what tool a company uses, but a round minute looks like a scheduler. It's the habit of a few pages: 7 companies published 51% of the :00 posts, and 27 of the 81 companies with at least 10 posts never used it. A post at :00 earns 0.97 times the company's usual.

How often

The median company posts 4.7 times a week, Schneider Electric 32, and Apple and Tesla posted nothing. Pages that post more earn less per follower, but that figure follows page size far more closely: its rank correlation is −0.78 with followers and −0.32 with posts per week. A page with ten times the followers gets about 2 times the reactions.

These are different companies, so the table can't say that posting less would earn more. Inside one company I can test it: if posts ate into each other, a post soon after the previous one would do worse. A post under 3 hours after the last one earns 1.01 times the company's usual.

The format

92% of posts carry an image, a video or a document, and video is the most common at 37%. Against the company's usual post, several images earn 1.35 and document carousels 0.75. One image (1.03) and video (1.00) are about usual. Articles read 1.37, but on 48 posts and with a wide range.

49% of posts name AI (or GenAI, LLMs, agentic, machine learning), and 50% carry a hashtag. Posts that name it earn 1.00 times the company's usual, and those that don't 1.00.
